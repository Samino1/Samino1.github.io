DesignedToWow Analytics

DesignedToWow Analytics is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channels Designed To Wow and Crafted To Wow.

It signs in with Google to read the statistics of these two channels (views, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources) through the YouTube Data API and the YouTube Analytics API, so the owner can see how each video performs and plan new videos. It also adds translated titles and descriptions to the owner's own videos.

The tool is not offered to other users. It does not post comments, upload videos or change anything on other people's channels.
