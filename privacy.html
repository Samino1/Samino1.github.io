Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This policy describes how DesignedToWow Analytics (the "App") handles data. The App is a private tool used only by the owner of the YouTube channels Designed To Wow and Crafted To Wow.

Data the App accesses

With the owner's permission through Google sign-in, the App reads data of the owner's own YouTube channels: video lists, titles, descriptions, view counts, watch time, audience retention, traffic sources and aggregated audience statistics (country, age group, device). It may update titles and descriptions of the owner's own videos with translations. It does not access data of other YouTube users.

How the data is used

The data is used only to show the owner how the channels' videos perform and to plan new videos.

Storage and sharing

Data is stored only on the owner's own computer. It is not sold, shared with or transferred to third parties, and it is not used for advertising.

Use of Google user data

The App's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. The App uses YouTube API Services; see the YouTube Terms of Service and the Google Privacy Policy.

Revoking access

Access can be revoked at any time in the Google Account under Security, Third-party apps with account access. Stored data is deleted on request.
